Robotics

  • mPragati, IIT DelhiSince 2026

    A vision-guided robot arm on servos with no encoders

    A 4-DOF arm that keeps a wrist camera on target: visual servoing, a coarse-fine split between joints, a digital twin still being improved, and a 6-DOF successor in CAD.

  • mPragati, IIT Delhi2026

    A 5:1 cycloidal reducer for a hobby servo

    A printed 5:1 drive for the arm’s next stage, analysed with my own contact model and a mesh-converged ANSYS check: load sharing, strength, backlash, efficiency and heat.

Biomechanics

  • mPragati, IIT DelhiSince 2026Page in progress

    Checking a patented elbow implant against its intended path

    Multi-body dynamics found the ulna’s path jumping at 90° of flexion. Then a hinge redesign, a test jig and a five-test protocol.

  • mPragati, IIT Delhi2026Page in progress

    Freeing a polycentric knee that couldn’t finish its flexion

    A four-bar knee that jammed, traced to its link proportions and resized until a printed prototype reached full flexion.

About

About

I’m in my third year of mechanical engineering at NSUT, New Delhi. I’m most interested in mechanisms that work with the human body: surgical tools, implants, prosthetics.

I like to sketch several options before choosing one, and to test designs in simulation before building them.

I also play the piano and take photographs.
