About me

About me

I’m a mechanical engineering student who has never really grown out of taking things apart to see how they work. (I know, I know, it’s a very common line… but it’s true.)

Most days that means designing something, trying to understand mechanisms, reading about the industry, surviving classes, doing my self-paced projects, etc. But that’s just one of the ways I look at the world.

Apart from this, I love admiring the beauty of nature and capturing it through a lens. Music is my escape from reality, a way to spend some time in a world of my own.

I also love talking to people, which is probably why I’d rather explain my work in person than document it on time 🙃

If you’re curious about something I’ve built, just ask <3
