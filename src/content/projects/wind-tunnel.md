## Overview

A tabletop subsonic wind tunnel with a 6:1 contraction, funded by an NSUT CPVSK project grant of ₹10,000. The design is complete, and optimisation and manufacturing are under way.

## What I did

- **Design calculations** for the tunnel sections.
- **CFD of the contraction** in ANSYS Fluent, to shape the nozzle for uniform flow in the test section.
- **Structural FEA** in ANSYS Static Structural, to cut wall thickness and printed mass without losing stiffness.
- **All of the electronics.**

## Now

Optimising the structural design to use less material, then manufacturing.

## What it taught me

How to trade off one analysis against another: every millimetre the FEA removed had to be checked against what the CFD needed.
