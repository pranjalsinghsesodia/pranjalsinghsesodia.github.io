## Overview

At the 2022 Change Makers bootcamp at IIT Delhi, I was one of 25 high-school students selected from across India. I led the 14-day mechanical assembly, servo actuation and gait programming of a quadruped walking robot with MIT Cheetah-inspired four-bar leg linkages.
