## Overview

I conceived and built EYEAR in school: a real-time device that translates speech into sign-language output, running on an ESP32 with an OLED display.

## Recognition

- Provisional patent application, India, 2022.
- Top 350 nationally, ATL Marathon 2021–22 (Atal Innovation Mission).
- Finalist, Smart India Hackathon Junior 2022.
- "Patent Pioneer" title from APS Shankar Vihar, as one of three students whose provisional filings made it the first of 137 Army Public Schools to file.
- Presented to the Union Minister of Education at IBM's national showcase in November 2022, during the IBM Under-18 Internship.
