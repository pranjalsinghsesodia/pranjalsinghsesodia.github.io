Total Elbow Implant Study

A patented total elbow implant is specified to move the ulna along an elliptical path. In simulation the path came out as two circular arcs with an offset jump at 90° of flexion, so I redesigned the hinge kinematics.

With
Dr. Dinesh Kalyanasundaram (supervisor); Yogesh Sahu (mentor)
Tool
ANSYS Multibody Dynamics
My part
Motion simulation, hinge kinematics redesign, test fixture design

Work

The work

The specification for this patented total elbow implant calls for an elliptical ulnar path with a 2-4 mm offset. I checked whether the joint actually moves that way.

Every image on this page shows iterative data. The final design could not be disclosed.

Method

How I checked it

I simulated the implant’s motion in ANSYS Multibody Dynamics and compared the path the ulna took with the one in the specification.

Outcome

What came out of it

The two paths didn’t match.

Specified ulnar pathSimulated ulnar path
ShapeEllipticalTwo circular arcs
Offset2-4 mmJumps at 90° of flexion

I then redesigned the hinge kinematics. The final design and the details of the redesign cannot be shared publicly.
